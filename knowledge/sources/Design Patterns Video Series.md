# Design Patterns Video Series

This catalog records the public [ArjanCodes Design Patterns playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLC0nd42SBTaNf0bVJVd9e2oBV-mcUuxS0). On 2026-09-19, all 32 entries were public and yielded timestamped, automatic English WebVTT captions. Local media, caption, metadata, and readable transcript files belong in the Git-ignored `.research/` directory.

The handbook uses these videos as learning sources, not as normative authority. The linked guidance is original and constrained by the handbook's principles and verification expectations.

This focused playlist overlaps with [[Software Design in Python Video Series]], but it remains a separate source because three entries on third-party boundaries, parameter objects, and policy pipelines are absent from the broader playlist.

## Verified lessons incorporated

- Video 1 shows protocol-backed, injected pricing behavior as an alternative to a hierarchy (12:49–14:53). This supports [[Strategy Injection]].
- Video 2 distinguishes an adapter, which changes one interface into another (9:54–10:00), from a facade that owns data, errors, and vocabulary crossing a third-party boundary (10:35–10:45). This informs [[External Service Boundary]].
- Video 4 moves recurring parameters and their validation into a query object (5:00–6:04). Video 15 warns that a parameter object can introduce coupling (18:55–19:00). Together they inform [[Validated Parameter Object]].
- Video 5 replaces flag-directed policy logic with a composable sequence of policy functions (4:40–9:17) and a names-to-functions registry (11:15–11:33). This informs [[Policy Pipeline]] and reinforces [[Registry-Based Selection]].
